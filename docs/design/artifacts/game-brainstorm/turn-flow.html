One roll, one decision cycle

The engine preserves the Python timing: after every eligible roll, all active players get a bank decision before the next roller acts.

Round state flow
Phase 1
Await roll

Human taps Roll, or controller advances an AI roller.

→
Phase 2
Roll resolved

Injected dice create pot and rule events.

→
Phase 3
Your decision

After safe roll 3: stay active or bank.

→
Phase 4
AI decisions

Strategies decide from the same state snapshot.

↘
Players remain

Rotate to the next active seat and return to Await roll.

Danger 7

Unbanked points vanish; emit RoundBusted.

Everyone banked

Close the round and advance.

What the human sees

When human rolls next
ROLL ON or BANK

When an AI rolls next
STAY IN or BANK

Before safe roll 3, no bank choice appears.

What the controller automates

AI thinking pause → decision reveal → next AI roll

If the human banks, AI play continues automatically so the round and match still resolve visibly.

Restart cancels the current animation sequence before resetting state.

Pure commands

START_GAME · ROLL_DICE · HUMAN_STAYS · HUMAN_BANKS · RESOLVE_AI_BANKING · ADVANCE_ROUND · RESTART

The three safe rolls use the same cycle but suppress banking until roll 3 has resolved.
